A personal learning
workspace.

A project for its owner and an invited undergraduate learner, bringing source materials, study and practice into a clearer learning path.

The system is being prepared around Google Drive, Gemini Notebook, Colab and AI Studio. Initial Google authorization and the first learner pilots are still being set up.

The intended learning cycle

01 / COLLECT

Start with sources.

Organize books, course materials and shared resources into a structured course or topic.

02 / PRACTISE

Work through the ideas.

Build toward source-grounded study chapters, executable labs and targeted practice.

03 / ADAPT

Learn from the gaps.

Use learner responses and evidence to refine practice and what comes next.

Google account access

Google authorization is used by a local Google Workspace CLI operated by the project owner. Requested access covers Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms, with account identity information for sign-in.

This website has no Google sign-in or application backend. The privacy policy describes the requested permissions, intended data handling and controls required before learning content is processed.
