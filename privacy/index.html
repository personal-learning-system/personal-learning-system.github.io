Personal Learning System privacy policy

The local Google Workspace CLI uses Google identity, email and profile information and account-wide permissions to read, create, edit and delete Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms content and metadata. Access is not limited to a learning folder. When learning workflows are enabled, source materials, work, answers and progress are used only for requested learning and resource-management features.

Planned learning resources stay in learners' Google accounts. Planned local processing retains curriculum, source and progress metadata and temporary files. Client secrets are managed in Proton Pass, with connected tools keeping their native configuration and authorization stores.

Google processes data held in its services. Planned learner-folder sharing gives the operator Editor access; Notebook and AI connections require separate authorization. Workspace data will not be sold, used for advertising, or used to train or improve general-purpose AI models, following Google's Limited Use requirements. AI transfers require learner consent and verification that the chosen service and account data-use controls meet those requirements.

Local track metadata is retained while the track is active; temporary files are removed when their task finishes, and review copies when accepted or superseded. On a track-closure request, project-held metadata and working copies are removed except deliverables the learner asks to keep. Learners control deletion of their Google files. Provider logs follow those providers' retention policies.

You can revoke Google access; this does not delete existing copies or native local authorization stores. Contact habileleo@gmail.com for removal requests or help. Material data-use changes require an updated notice and any additional consent before new processing.

This site has no sign-in, forms, analytics or tracking scripts and receives no Google credentials or learning content. GitHub Pages records visitor IP addresses for security under GitHub's privacy statement.
