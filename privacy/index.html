Privacy policy

Prepared 16 November 2026

Personal Learning System is being prepared for its owner and an invited learner. This policy sets the conditions for activating its planned Google connections and learning workflows. Contact the operator at habileleo@gmail.com.

Google access

Once authorized, the proposed Google Workspace CLI connection will permit access to:

  • Google account identity, primary email address and profile information for sign-in.
  • Reading, creating, editing and deleting content through Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms bodies, including file metadata.
  • When learning workflows are enabled: source materials, study work, learner answers and progress evidence used for the requested learning activity.

The proposed OAuth permissions are broad account permissions. They are not restricted by Google to a single Learning folder. Gemini Notebook and AI tools are separate planned integrations; these Workspace permissions do not authorize them. Enabling those integrations requires their own authorization and notice of any additional processing.

Purpose and AI processing

Google data is used for the learning and resource-management features the learner requests. Personal Learning System commits to Google's Limited Use requirements: this data will not be sold, used for advertising, or used to train or improve general-purpose AI models. AI-assisted authoring is planned through Google AI services; Workspace-derived content will only be sent after learner consent and verification that the chosen service and account data-use settings meet those requirements.

Storage

Planned learning resources and generated work will be kept in the learners' Google Drive and Gemini Notebook accounts when those connections are enabled. The local control plane will retain curriculum and source metadata, progress state and temporary processing files. Client secrets will be managed in Proton Pass, with each connected tool maintaining its native local configuration and authorization store.

Sharing

Google will process the account and learning data held in its services. The planned learner setup gives the operator Editor access to the learner's designated learning folder for authoring and support. Additional transfer to an AI processing service requires the learner consent and data-use checks described above.

Retention and deletion

For an activated learning track, local curriculum and progress metadata will be retained while the track is active. Temporary processing files will be removed when their task finishes; copies delivered for review will remain until accepted or superseded. On a learner's request to close a track, the operator will remove project-held metadata and working copies, except deliverables the learner asks to keep. Learners control deletion of their Google files. You can revoke third-party access in your Google Account; this does not delete existing copies or the local authorization store, which require separate removal. Contact the operator for that help. Service-provider logs follow those providers' retention policies.

This website

The public pages have no sign-in, forms, analytics or tracking scripts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which records visitor IP addresses for security under GitHub's privacy statement. This static website does not receive Google OAuth credentials or learning content.

Contact

For questions about access, consent or removal of project-held data, email habileleo@gmail.com. Material changes to data use will be reflected in this policy and require any additional consent before that new use begins.
